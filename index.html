Behind Kitten Gems

Meet Maira, the Maker & Designer

Kitten Gems started out of a deep-seated passion for delicate craftsmanship, gemstones, and playful patterns. Disheartened by mass-manufactured, quickly tarnished jewelry, founder Maira set out to handcraft custom accessories that represent personality, beauty, and resilience.

Every single piece is completely unique. From hand-bending high-quality gold-plated brass hoops to setting natural mother-of-pearl nuggets, Maira infuses warmth, heritage, and modern e-commerce quality into each drop.

“I believe that jewelry is a personal signature. Whether it is a colorful glass cherry bead set or refined raw pearls, it should evoke delight and confidence.”
